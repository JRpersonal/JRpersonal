### Hi, I'm Jens 👋

I write software in Germany and I like to see how far a good idea can go when it gets built properly.

On 6 May 2026 Bose switched off the SoundTouch cloud, and millions of perfectly good speakers forgot how to play the radio overnight. I took that a little personally. So I built **[SoundTouch Reborn](https://github.com/JRpersonal/streborn)** and brought them back, without the Bose cloud and without an account.

#### 🧪 How I work

Six speakers on my desk as a test lab, a lot of reverse engineering, and AI as my pair programmer. The bar stays where a good team would put it: 1,400+ tests, signed and notarized releases, 13 languages, and a community that finds bugs faster than I can make coffee.

#### 🔧 Things I brought back to life

| | |
|---|---|
| **[streborn](https://github.com/JRpersonal/streborn)** | Bose SoundTouch without the Bose cloud. A small Go agent on the speaker, a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, a phone remote. |
| **[streborn-doom](https://github.com/JRpersonal/streborn-doom)** | Purely for science 😉 The original DOOM on a SoundTouch: its tiny display, its IR remote, its speaker. If it has a screen, it has to run DOOM. Those are the rules. [#ItRunsDoom](https://github.com/topics/itrunsdoom) |
| **[spacemonger](https://github.com/JRpersonal/spacemonger)** | The classic 90s disk space treemap, now on x64 and ARM64. I may have a thing for old stuff that deserves a second life. |
